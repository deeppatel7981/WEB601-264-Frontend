Welcome to Seattle Pet Adoption Center

Since 2015, Seattle Pet Adoption Center has been dedicated to finding loving homes for abandoned and rescued animals in Seattle, Washington. Our mission is to connect pets with caring families while promoting responsible pet ownership.

Our Mission

We provide a safe, nurturing environment for homeless dogs while working tirelessly to match them with their forever families. Every year, we rescue and rehome over 500 wonderful dogs of all breeds and sizes.

Why Adopt?

  • Save a life and give a pet a second chance
  • All pets are vaccinated, spayed/neutered, and microchipped
  • Adoption counselors help match you with the perfect companion
  • Post-adoption support and resources included
  • Lower cost compared to purchasing from breeders

Adoption Process

  1. Browse our available pets
  2. Submit an adoption application online or in-person
  3. Meet with an adoption counselor for an interview
  4. Spend time with your potential new pet
  5. Complete the adoption and bring your pet home

The entire process typically takes 2-3 days. Adoption fees range from $50 to $150 depending on the animal.
